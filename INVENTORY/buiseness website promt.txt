Full Builder Prompt for Site and Dashboard
Project Summary
Company: Genum Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Business model: Online-first e-commerce + services marketplace for robotics, automation, 3D printing, and 2D printing.
Primary goals: Sell kits & components; offer 3D/2D printing services; run workshops and school packages; provide an easy management dashboard that keeps product stock and service schedules in sync.

Deliverables
1. Responsive website with Home, Shop, Courses, Services, About, Blog, Contact, Support/FAQ.
2. Product & Service catalog pages with category pages, product detail pages, and cross-sell suggestions.
3. Admin management dashboard for inventory, orders, service scheduling, and reports.
4. CSV / Excel export of all products and services in upload-ready format.
5. Sample content (homepage hero text, 3 product descriptions, About Us copy, 3 blog post outlines).
6. Integration plan for payment gateways, calendar sync, and automation (Zapier/Make).
7. Design assets list (color palette, logo concept, font suggestions, image placeholders).

Must Keep
- Core categories: Robotics kits & components; Controllers & boards; Motors & motion; Sensors & modules; Power & charging; Mechanical parts & fasteners; Pre-packaged kits; 3D printing services; 2D printing press services; Teaching & workshops.
- Branding: Nepal-inspired palette (sky blue, Everest white, crimson red, navy blue).
- CTAs: Buy Now, Add to Cart, Book Service, Join Workshop, Request Quote.
- Customer types: Students, schools, institutions, hobbyists.
- Management features: Inventory sync, order statuses, service scheduling, role-based access, weekly reports.

Remove / Avoid
- Placeholder or lorem ipsum text.
- Duplicate categories or repeated product listings.
- Unused widgets or social feeds with no content.
- Overly broad objectives — focus on core services and product lines.

Site Structure Requirements
Home: Hero, featured categories, how it works, testimonials, footer.
Shop: Filters (Category, Subcategory, Price, Supplier, Availability), grid view, category landing pages.
PDP: Images + gallery + 3D viewer for printable parts, title, SKU, short description, price, stock, related components with separate price tags, add-ons, specs, supplier, warranty, downloadable resources, reviews & Q&A.
Services/Courses: Service cards, booking calendar, file upload for design files, pricing calculator for 3D prints.
About: Mission, vision, team, partnerships.
Blog: Tutorials and educational content.
Contact/Support/FAQ: Form, email, phone, ticketing/chatbot.

Admin Dashboard Requirements
- Inventory management with real-time stock, low-stock alerts, supplier field per SKU.
- Order management with statuses and customer portal.
- Service scheduling with calendar view and Google/Outlook sync.
- Product ↔ Service linking (prompt to book related service after purchase).
- Role-based access: Admin, Staff, Viewer.
- Reports & exports: Sales, inventory movement, service utilization; CSV export.
- Automations: Zapier/Make flows to update Google Sheets, send emails, sync calendar.

UX UI Guidelines
- Colors: Primary sky blue #4DA8E2; Accent crimson red #C62828; Neutral Everest white #F7FAFF; Dark navy #0B2545.
- Typography: Headings Poppins; Body Inter.
- Buttons: High contrast, rounded corners.
- Mobile: Sticky bottom CTA for Buy/Book; collapsible specs on PDP.
- Accessibility: Alt text, keyboard navigable, color contrast >= WCAG AA.

Integrations & Tech Stack
- Platform: WordPress + WooCommerce OR Next.js + Shopify/WooCommerce API.
- Payments: eSewa, Khalti (Nepal); PayPal, Stripe (global).
- Calendar: Google Calendar / Outlook sync.
- Automation: Zapier or Make.
- 3D Viewer: model-viewer or Sketchfab embed.
- Hosting: Vercel (Next.js) or managed WordPress hosting.
- Design assets: Figma file; Canva templates.

Product Display Rules
1. Hierarchy: Category → Product → Related Components.
2. Each product must list related components with separate price fields and SKU.
3. Add-on pricing: allow customers to add components individually to cart.
4. Bundle option: show kit price and itemized breakdown.
5. Supplier field: show supplier name on PDP.
6. Stock control: warn if related component out of stock and show restock estimate.

Pricing & Shipping Rules
- Prices in NPR by default; multi-currency display optional.
- Shipping: Domestic flat rates + weight-based; optional international quotes.
- Service pricing: hourly or per-session for workshops; per-piece or per-gram for 3D prints.

SEO & Content
- Meta title & description for every page.
- Structured data: Product, Offer, Review, LocalBusiness.
- Blog: 2–3 SEO articles to start.
- Image alt text and compressed images.

CSV / Excel Import Template
Header row (one-line cells):
SKU,Title,Category,Subcategory,Short Description,Price_NPR,Stock,Supplier,Related_SKUs,Weight_g,Material,Dimensions_mm,Image_URL,Tags,Downloadable_File_URL

Excel Import Instruction
- Related_SKUs: comma-separated SKUs.
- SKU pattern: GEN-<CATEGORYCODE>-<4DIGIT> (e.g., GEN-ARD-0001).
- Map the provided Robotics quotation.xlsx to this template and preserve supplier names.

Acceptance Criteria
- All core pages implemented and responsive.
- Shop with filters and PDPs showing related components and separate prices.
- Admin dashboard with inventory sync, order statuses, and calendar scheduling.
- CSV export/import works and matches template.
- Payment gateways configured (test mode).
- Basic SEO meta tags and 3 starter blog posts.
- Figma or design file included.

Management Tool Requirements
Dashboard Features
- Product status tracking: real-time stock levels for kits, components, and printing services.
- Order management: pending, in-progress, completed; automatic notifications.
- Service scheduling: calendar view for teaching sessions and printing deadlines; Google Calendar/Outlook sync.
- Reports: weekly/monthly sales and service usage.
- Role-based access: Admin, Staff, Customer Portal.
- Inventory sync: auto-update when sales occur.
- Service sync: auto-update when bookings are made.
- Automations: Zapier/Make to link WooCommerce → Google Sheets → Calendar.

Workflow Example
1. Customer buys a kit → stock auto-decreases.
2. Customer books a 3D printing job → service calendar auto-updates.
3. Dashboard shows products in stock and services scheduled side-by-side.
4. Weekly report auto-emailed.
