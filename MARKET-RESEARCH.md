# GENUM market research

Research checked 19 August 2026 against the public Himalayan Solution website:

- https://himalayansolution.com/
- https://himalayansolution.com/shop
- https://himalayansolution.com/about-us

## Observed market categories

Himalayan Solution publicly presents electronics components, development boards, sensors, modules, wireless/IoT, embedded systems, robotics kits, motors and wheels, batteries, ICs, switches, tools, industrial automation, STEM learning kits, and 3D-printing parts. Their catalog also surfaces Reprap printer thermistors and other printer maintenance components.

## GENUM response

GENUM should not copy product names, images, prices, page structure, or brand language. The opportunity is to differentiate through:

- Curated project bundles instead of a very broad parts catalog
- Nepali and English quickstart guides
- Print-to-order 3D fabrication and design-for-print support
- AI + IoT learning labs with human-centered evaluation
- Beginner-friendly product selection and build advice
- Transparent stock, delivery, warranty, and pre-order states
- Workshops connected directly to the products customers buy

## Initial product plan

1. Keep robotics kits, sensor kits, motors, and classroom products as the core.
2. Add PLA filament and printer-care supplies as the first 3D-printing commerce layer.
3. Add print-to-order prototypes, small-batch parts, and CAD review as services.
4. Add edge AI and IoT labs as clearly marked pre-orders until stock and support are real.
5. Use Strapi to expand the catalog only after supplier cost, landed cost, warranty, and Nepal delivery time are verified.

This is market positioning research, not an affiliation or endorsement by Himalayan Solution.
